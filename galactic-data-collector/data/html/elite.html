    3. Elite

    Star Wars Elite action figures are produced by Disney and are made of die cast metal. They are not numbered or released in waves like the Black Series. The Elite Premium series is new for 2016 and are taller in size (11-12") and are made of plastic, but feature 30 points of articulation.
