    Star Wars Titanium Black Series consists of small die cast action figures, vehicles and helmets. Vehicles are sold individually, but helmets come two in a pack. The figures include a double sided backdrop and character specific accessory. They are numbered and are released in waves like the 6" Black Series line of figures.
